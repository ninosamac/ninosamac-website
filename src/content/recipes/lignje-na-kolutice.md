---
title: Lignje na kolutiće
date: 2026-10-27
description: >-
  Crisp fried squid rings, the Adriatic konoba classic — a light coating, a hot
  pan, and lemon.
servings: 4
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 15
ingredients:
  - 800 g cleaned squid (tubes, tentacles optional)
  - 150 g plain flour
  - 50 g fine semolina or cornflour, for extra crunch
  - 1 tsp salt, plus more to finish
  - Black pepper
  - 500 ml neutral oil, for frying
  - 2 lemons
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 small bunch parsley, finely chopped
  - Tartar sauce or aioli, to serve (optional)
steps:
  - Pat the squid dry. Slice the tubes into rings about 1 cm wide and leave the tentacles in pairs or small clusters. Keep them dry — damp squid spits and steams instead of crisping.
  - Mix the flour, semolina, salt, and a generous grind of pepper in a wide bowl.
  - Heat the oil in a deep pan to 180 °C. A pinch of the flour mix should sizzle and float straight away.
  - Toss a handful of rings in the flour, shake off the excess in a sieve, and lower them into the oil. Work in small batches so the oil stays hot.
  - Fry for 1½–2 minutes until pale gold. Squid turns rubbery if cooked longer, so err on the short side.
  - Lift out with a slotted spoon onto paper, salt straight away, and keep warm while you fry the rest, letting the oil come back to 180 °C between batches.
  - Toss the rings with the garlic and parsley, and serve at once with lemon wedges and, if you like, tartar sauce or aioli.

---

Squid wants either a very short cook or a very long one; this is the very short
one. Eat it within minutes of leaving the pan, because the coating goes soft as
it sits.
