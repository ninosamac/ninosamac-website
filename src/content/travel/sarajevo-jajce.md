1 January 2026. A late-morning stop at the Sebilj in Sarajevo's Baščaršija, then
on to Jajce for the afternoon — the Pliva falling straight through the middle
of town, the old town climbing the hill above it, and the streets lighting up
around the gate tower by evening.
