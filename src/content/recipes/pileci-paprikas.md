---
title: Pileći paprikaš
date: 2026-09-03
description: >-
  Bone-in chicken braised with onion and paprika, finished with sour cream
  stabilised so it will not split.
servings: 4
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 55
ingredients:
  - 1.5 kg bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs and drumsticks
  - 2 large onions (about 400 g), finely chopped
  - 3 tbsp lard or neutral oil
  - 3 tbsp sweet Hungarian paprika (not smoked)
  - 1 red bell pepper, finely diced
  - 1 tomato, grated (or 2 tbsp tomato paste)
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 250 ml chicken stock or water
  - 200 ml full-fat sour cream
  - 1 tbsp plain flour
  - Salt and black pepper
steps:
  - Season the chicken with salt. Heat the lard in a wide heavy pot over medium-high heat and brown the pieces on all sides, then set them aside.
  - Lower the heat to medium. Cook the onions with a pinch of salt for 10–15 minutes until soft and golden. Add the garlic and diced pepper and cook for 3 minutes.
  - Off the heat, stir in the paprika and grated tomato so the paprika does not scorch. Return to low heat and stir for 30 seconds.
  - Return the chicken and its juices. Add the stock — it should come about halfway up the meat. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  - Cover and simmer for 35–40 minutes, turning the pieces once, until the chicken is tender and pulling from the bone.
  - Whisk the flour into the sour cream until smooth. Temper it by stirring in a few spoonfuls of the hot sauce, then pour the mixture back into the pot.
  - Simmer uncovered for 5 minutes, stirring, until the sauce thickens and coats a spoon. Do not let it boil hard or it may split. Adjust the salt.
tags:
  - hungarian
  - chicken
---

Skin-on, bone-in matters — it is where the body of the sauce comes from. The
sour cream must be full fat and tempered, or it curdles. Keep it clean: onion,
paprika, chicken, cream, and no caraway or marjoram. Serve with nokedli or wide
egg noodles and a sharp pickled cucumber.
