---
title: Fritule
date: 2026-08-24
description: >-
  Small Dalmatian fritters with rum, citrus zest, and raisins — Christmas-market
  food any time of year.
hero: ./fritule.webp
heroCredit: "Photo: Klenje, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons"
servings: 6
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 20
ingredients:
  - 300 g plain flour
  - 2 eggs
  - 60 g sugar, plus more for dusting
  - 200 ml milk, lukewarm
  - 50 g raisins, soaked in 30 ml rum
  - Zest of 1 lemon and 1 orange
  - 1 tsp baking powder
  - Pinch of salt
  - 1 l neutral oil, for frying
  - Icing sugar, to finish
steps:
  - Whisk the eggs and sugar until pale. Whisk in the milk, citrus zest, and the rum from the soaked raisins.
  - Fold in the flour, baking powder, and salt to a thick, sticky batter — closer to a drop-scone mix than a dough. Stir through the raisins.
  - Rest the batter, covered, for 30 minutes at room temperature.
  - Heat the oil to 170 °C in a deep pan. Test with a small blob of batter — it should sink, then surface within a couple of seconds.
  - Using two teaspoons, drop walnut-sized pieces into the oil in batches. Fry for 3–4 minutes, turning, until deep gold and cooked through.
  - Drain on paper. Dust heavily with icing sugar and eat warm.
tags:

---

The batter is forgiving: too loose and the fritule spread, too stiff and they
stay doughy in the middle. Adjust with a spoon of flour or milk after the first
test fritter.
