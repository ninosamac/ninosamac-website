It is out. **The Greek Thinkers** is finally on YouTube, and I am proud of it.

Ten episodes of about an hour each, in chronological order: from Thales and the
first philosophers around 600 BC, through Pythagoras, Socrates, Plato and
Aristotle, to Archimedes, the Roman Stoics, and the closing of the Athenian
schools in AD 529. Slow, quiet, and made to be listened to in the evening.

## How it feels to finish

I started this as a way to learn how to make videos at all. The
[Faraday lectures](/blog/faraday-candle-lectures) were the pilot, and I wrote
down what I learned [along the way](/blog/learning-to-make-youtube-videos).
Somewhere in the middle there were a lot of overnight image batches, a lot of
listening to one sentence over and over, and a few moments of wondering whether
a ten-hour series was a sensible thing to do on the side.

It was. Seeing the whole playlist sitting there, in order, is a very good
feeling.

## What comes next

The scripts are reviewed by me, the voice and the paintings are made on my own
machine, and every episode is an overnight batch. It works, but it is slow.

That is exactly why the AI rig is coming. When it arrives, work like this should
get out **much faster**: more episodes, more series, less waiting. I am already
looking forward to it, and I will write about it when it is alive.

For now: pour something, find a quiet hour, and start with Thales.
