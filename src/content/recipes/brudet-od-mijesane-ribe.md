---
title: Brudet od miješane ribe
date: 2026-10-27
description: >-
  Dalmatian mixed fish stew — onion, garlic, tomato and white wine, simmered
  gently so the fish stays whole.
hero: ./brudet-od-mijesane-ribe.webp
heroCredit: "Photo: Chiqui Teo, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons"
servings: 4
prepMinutes: 25
cookMinutes: 40
ingredients:
  - 1 kg mixed fish, cleaned and cut into thick pieces (e.g. scorpion fish, monkfish, conger, gurnard, John Dory)
  - 4 tbsp olive oil
  - 2 medium onions, finely sliced
  - 4 cloves garlic, minced
  - 2 tbsp tomato paste
  - 400 g ripe tomatoes, peeled and chopped (or a tin of chopped tomatoes)
  - 150 ml dry white wine
  - 1 bay leaf
  - 1 small bunch parsley, chopped
  - 150 ml fish stock or water
  - Salt and black pepper
  - Polenta or crusty bread, to serve
steps:
  - Salt the fish lightly and leave it at room temperature while you start the base.
  - Heat the olive oil in a wide heavy pan over medium heat. Soften the onions for 10–12 minutes until golden and sweet, then add the garlic for one minute.
  - Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes. Add the wine and let it bubble down by half.
  - Add the tomatoes, bay leaf, half the parsley and the stock. Simmer gently for 15 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  - Lay the firmest fish in first (conger, monkfish, scorpion fish) and cook for 8 minutes, then add the more delicate pieces and cook for another 6–8 minutes.
  - Do not stir with a spoon. Shake the pan now and then so the fish does not break up, and spoon the sauce over it if it looks dry.
  - Season, scatter the remaining parsley, rest for 5 minutes and serve with polenta or bread.

Brudet is better the more kinds of fish go into it, because each gives something
different to the sauce. Ask the fishmonger for a mix and for the heads and bones,
and simmer them in the stock if you have the time. It is even better the next
day.
