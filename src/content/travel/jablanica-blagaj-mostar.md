30 December 2025, a day trip down the Neretva valley. A stop at Jablanica for the
bombed railway bridge and the old steam locomotive beside it, then the Buna
spring and its dervish house under the cliff at Blagaj, and the afternoon in
Mostar — the Crooked Bridge, the Old Bridge and the light going on the river.
