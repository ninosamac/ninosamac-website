27–28 June 2026. Saturday on the Istrian coast — Rovinj's waterfront from a
wooden excursion boat, a green bay, and an evening in a small harbour town.
Sunday up on Velika Planina, the high pasture of the Kamnik Alps, among the
herdsmen's huts, cows and wooden benches.
