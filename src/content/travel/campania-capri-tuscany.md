4–7 June 2026. A day at Pompeii and an evening in Naples, a boat day along the
Amalfi Coast through Amalfi and Positano, another on Capri — the harbour at
Marina Grande, the garden terraces and the view of the Faraglioni — and a last
stop in a Tuscan hill town on the way home.
