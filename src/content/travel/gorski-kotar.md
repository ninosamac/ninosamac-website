July 2023, based up in the hills. Lake Lokve and the motorway viaduct the first
evening; a day in Golubinjak — spruce forest, the Pigeon Cave and the rock arch
they call the Gates of Hell — then the Kamačnik canyon boardwalk and dinner at
the tavern. A day trip down to Rijeka for the harbour and the Dead Channel, and
on the way out the old turreted villa at Severin na Kupi.
