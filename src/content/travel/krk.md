27 August 2023, a day out to Krk. Morning at Punat — one of the biggest
marinas on the Adriatic — then the little boat over to Košljun, the wooded
islet in the bay with a Franciscan monastery and its crowded museum of ships,
shells and birds. In the afternoon a longer excursion down the coast, past the
cliffs to a cove for a swim, and back to Punat by evening.
