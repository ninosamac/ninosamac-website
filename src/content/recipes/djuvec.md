  Balkan rice baked with pork, peppers, tomatoes and carrots — a one-pan
  supper in a red, paprika-scented sauce.

servings: 6
prepMinutes: 25
cookMinutes: 60
ingredients:
  - 700 g pork shoulder, cut into 2 cm cubes
  - 3 tbsp oil or lard
  - 2 large onions, chopped
  - 3 cloves garlic, minced
  - 2 carrots, diced
  - 2 bell peppers (red and green), cut into strips
  - 1 tbsp sweet paprika
  - 1 tbsp tomato paste
  - 400 g chopped tomatoes, tinned or fresh
  - 300 g long-grain rice, rinsed
  - 700 ml hot water or light stock
  - 1 bay leaf
  - 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  - Black pepper
  - 100 g frozen peas (optional)
  - Chopped parsley, to finish
steps:
  - Season the pork with salt and pepper. Heat the oil in a wide ovenproof pot over medium-high heat and brown the pork in two batches for about 5 minutes each. Set aside.
  - In the same oil, soften the onions for 8–10 minutes, then add the garlic and carrots and cook for 3 minutes.
  - Take the pot off the heat, stir in the paprika and tomato paste, then return to the heat. Add the peppers, tomatoes and bay leaf and return the pork to the pot. Cook for 10 minutes.
  - Stir in the rice, hot water and salt. Bring to a simmer.
  - Cover and bake at 180 °C for 40 minutes, stirring once after 25 minutes, until the rice has absorbed the liquid and the pork is tender. Add the peas for the last 10 minutes, if using. If it looks dry, add a splash of hot water.
  - Rest for 10 minutes, scatter with parsley, and serve with a shopska salad or pickles.

Đuveč is named after the clay pot it was traditionally baked in, and it is the
dish that every Balkan family makes a bit differently — some add sausage or
aubergine, some use chicken. Do not stir it much once the rice goes in, or it
turns sticky. Add the paprika off the heat so it does not burn and turn bitter.
It reheats well the next day with a splash of water.
