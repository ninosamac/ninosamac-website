1–8 August 2026. An August week through central Italy — Siena and its Campo
and striped cathedral, a Tuscan afternoon in San Gimignano, a day in Florence
around the Uffizi, the Piazza della Signoria and the Arno, and a last day in
Ferrara walking around the Castello Estense.
