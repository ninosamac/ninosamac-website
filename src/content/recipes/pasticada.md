title: Pašticada
date: 2026-11-08
description: >-
  Dalmatian festive beef, marinated overnight in vinegar and larded with
  garlic and bacon, then braised for hours in a dark, sweet-sour sauce with
  prošek and dried plums.
hero: ./pasticada.webp
heroCredit: "Photo: Popo le Chien, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons"
servings: 8
prepMinutes: 45
cookMinutes: 210
ingredients:
  - 1.5 kg beef round or topside, in one piece
  - 100 g pancetta or smoked bacon, cut into thin strips
  - 6 cloves garlic, 4 sliced into slivers and 2 minced
  - 6 cloves, 1 cinnamon stick, 1 small bunch rosemary
  - 250 ml red wine vinegar (wine vinegar, not spirit vinegar)
  - 3 tbsp olive oil
  - 2 onions, finely chopped
  - 2 carrots, finely chopped
  - 1 celery stalk, finely chopped
  - 2 tbsp tomato paste
  - 250 ml prošek (or another sweet dessert wine)
  - 250 ml dry red wine
  - 10 dried plums, stoned
  - 2 bay leaves
  - 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  - Black pepper and freshly grated nutmeg
  - Gnocchi or homemade pasta, to serve
steps:
  - Make deep slits all over the beef with a thin sharp knife and push a sliver of garlic and a strip of pancetta into each one. Stud the meat with the cloves.
  - Put the beef in a bowl with the vinegar and rosemary, turn to coat, cover and refrigerate for 12–24 hours, turning once or twice.
  - Lift the beef out, pat it dry and season with salt and pepper. Keep the marinade.
  - Heat the olive oil in a heavy casserole just big enough for the meat. Brown the beef on all sides over medium-high heat, about 10 minutes, then set it aside.
  - In the same pot soften the onions, carrots and celery over medium heat for 12–15 minutes until golden. Add the minced garlic for one minute, then stir in the tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes.
  - Return the beef to the pot with the cinnamon, bay leaves, prošek, red wine, the marinade and enough water to come halfway up the meat. Bring to a simmer, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 2½ hours, turning the meat every 40 minutes.
  - Add the dried plums, nutmeg and extra salt to taste. Cook for another 45 minutes with the lid slightly ajar, until the meat is very tender and the sauce has reduced.
  - Lift the meat out and rest it for 15 minutes. Remove the bay leaves and cinnamon and purée the sauce with a stick blender until smooth and glossy, thinning with a splash of hot water if it is too thick.
  - Slice the beef thickly across the grain, spoon the sauce over it and serve with gnocchi or pasta.

Pašticada is the dish of weddings, Easter and Sunday in Dalmatia, and it is
started the day before. The vinegar marinade is what tenderises the beef and
gives the sauce its sweet-sour depth, so do not shorten it. No flour is needed:
the vegetables and plums melt into the sauce and blending it makes it thick.
It is better the next day, reheated gently.
