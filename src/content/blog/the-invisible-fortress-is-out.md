## Where to start

If you want the long way round, start with episode 1 and its clay tablet. If you
build or run payments and logins, episodes 4 to 6 on PSD2 and multi-factor
authentication are the ones closest to the daily work, and episodes 10 and 11
cover the quantum threat and the slow migration away from today's cryptography.

Every factual claim in the narration is tied to a source I read, and where a
framing is my own the series says so. The narration is a synthetic voice and the
illustrations are AI-generated, and the videos are labelled that way on YouTube.
Educational overview, not legal or financial advice.

Thank you for watching, and tell me what I got wrong.
