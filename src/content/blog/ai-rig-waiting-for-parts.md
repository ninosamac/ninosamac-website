I had been putting this off for a while, and in the end I just bought it.
Renting compute is fine for experiments. But the projects I want to build need
a machine that is always on, always mine, and does not send me a bill every
time I get curious. And waiting for prices to drop is much like waiting for Godot.

So: an AI rig.

## The plan

- A quiet, headless Linux box that runs 24/7
- AMD Ryzen 9 7900 on an AM5 board
- Two RTX 5060 Ti cards with 16 GB each, which makes 32 GB of VRAM
- Room to grow, because the board has slots for more and I know myself

## The state of things

Ordered, paid, and in transit. The motherboard is somewhere in the DPD
network, the CPU and case are waiting for pickup, and I have refreshed the
tracking page more times than I'd like to admit.

## What I want to do with it

- Run models locally, all day, without watching a meter
- Keep making slow, quiet videos, like the
  [Faraday lectures](/blog/faraday-candle-lectures), with a pipeline that lives
  on my own desk
- Try things that would be too silly to pay for by the hour

The parts arrive in the first week of November. After that it's BIOS, Ubuntu
Server, GPU drivers, and the first model answering from a machine I built.

I'll write it up when it's alive.
