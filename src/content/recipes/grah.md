title: Grah

description: >-
  Croatian bean stew with smoked pork and sausage, simmered until the broth
  turns thick and sweet — winter lunch with bread and pickles.
hero: ./grah.webp
heroCredit: "Photo: Ivana Sokolović, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons"
servings: 6
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 120
ingredients:
  - 500 g dried white beans (or borlotti), soaked overnight
  - 400 g smoked pork ribs or a smoked pork hock
  - 200 g smoked sausage (kobasica), sliced
  - 2 tbsp oil or lard
  - 2 onions, chopped
  - 3 cloves garlic, minced
  - 2 carrots, diced
  - 1 celery stalk, diced
  - 2 tbsp tomato paste
  - 2 tsp sweet paprika
  - 2 bay leaves
  - 1.5 litres water
  - Salt and black pepper
  - Chopped parsley and a splash of vinegar, to finish
steps:
  - Drain the soaked beans, put them in a pot with the smoked ribs and the water, bring to the boil and skim. Simmer gently for 45 minutes, without salt.
  - Meanwhile heat the oil in a pan and soften the onions for 10 minutes. Add the garlic, carrots and celery and cook for 5 minutes. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the paprika and tomato paste, then return it to the heat for 1 minute.
  - Stir the vegetables into the beans, add the bay leaves and sausage, and simmer for another 45–60 minutes, until the beans are tender and the broth is thick.
  - Lift out the ribs. Mash a ladleful of beans against the side of the pot to thicken the broth, and pull the meat off the bones and return it to the pot.
  - Season with salt and pepper. Stir in a splash of vinegar, scatter with parsley, and rest for 10 minutes.
  - Serve in deep bowls with crusty bread, raw onion or pickled peppers.

Grah is Monday-lunch food, cooked in a big pot and eaten for two days. Add the
salt late, because salted water keeps beans hard, and mash a few beans into the
broth instead of making a roux — they thicken it and keep the flavour clean. A
splash of vinegar at the end lifts the smoky, sweet broth. It tastes better the
next day.
