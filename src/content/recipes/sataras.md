  Slowly stewed peppers, tomatoes and onion, finished with eggs — a quick
  Croatian and Serbian supper that is also a side dish.

servings: 4
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 35
ingredients:
  - 3 tbsp oil or lard
  - 2 large onions, sliced
  - 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  - 5 bell peppers (a mix of green and red, or long pale peppers), cut into strips
  - 600 g ripe tomatoes, peeled and chopped (or a tin of chopped tomatoes)
  - 1 tsp sweet paprika
  - 1 tsp sugar
  - 150 g smoked sausage (kulen or kobasica), sliced (optional)
  - 4 eggs
  - Salt and black pepper
  - Fresh bread, to serve
steps:
  - Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium heat and cook the onions for 10 minutes until soft and golden. Add the garlic for one minute.
  - Add the sausage, if using, and cook for 3 minutes until it starts to colour.
  - Take the pan off the heat and stir in the paprika, then add the peppers and return to the heat. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until they begin to soften.
  - Add the tomatoes, sugar and salt. Cook uncovered over medium-low heat for 15 minutes until the vegetables are tender and the sauce is thick, not watery.
  - Lightly beat the eggs and pour them over the sataraš. Stir gently for 1–2 minutes until just set, or stop stirring and let them cook into soft curds. Season with pepper and serve at once with bread.

Sataraš is made all summer when peppers and tomatoes are cheap, and it can be
a side for grilled meat or a supper with only bread. Without the sausage and the
eggs it is a plain vegetable stew that keeps for days, so cook a double batch
and add the eggs to each portion when you reheat it. Add the paprika off the heat
so it does not turn bitter, and add a pinch of sugar if the tomatoes are sharp.
