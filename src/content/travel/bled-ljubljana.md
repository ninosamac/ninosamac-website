An old set from May 2023 — day one of a longer trip. Morning on Lake Bled: the
20-minute row out to the island, the 99 steps, and the walk around the
shoreline under the castle. Afternoon in Ljubljana along the river, from the
Dragon Bridge down past the market colonnade.
