An afternoon in Split at the end of November. Up to Marjan first for the view
back over the old town and the harbour, then down to Matejuška, where the small
boats sit out the winter, and east along the Riva in the last of the sun.

The final frame is from a week later, in early December, when the Advent
decorations had gone up along the waterfront.
