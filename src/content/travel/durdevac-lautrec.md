A June 2023 day trip into Podravina. The reason to go was the Toulouse-Lautrec
lithograph show inside the Stari grad — the moated brick fortress in the middle
of Đurđevac — which also had a LEGO model of the fort's own siege. Then a stop
at the camel farm, and the afternoon on Koprivnica's main square.
