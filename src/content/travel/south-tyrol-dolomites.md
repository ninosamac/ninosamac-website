21–23 August 2026. A late-summer run through South Tyrol — an evening by Lago
di Braies under low cloud, a morning in the Trauttmansdorff gardens above
Merano followed by Bolzano's cathedral, Brixen's old town on Sunday morning,
and the afternoon in the Dolomites around Lago di Misurina with the Tre Cime
di Lavaredo in the clouds.
